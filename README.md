# 17 @ 16

This is a virtual novel game based on my own experience as a teen volunteer in Yonkers, New York. The game features 16-year-old Ada, an about-to-be high school graduate in the greater NYC area looking to find volunteer opportunties to meet her graduation requirements. Follow Ada's journey by learning more about real programs for teens in Yonkers through the lens of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDG), with each of the 17 levels focusing on one of the UN's goals.

I represented famous women in computer science in this game as well:
Ada Lovelace - Ada (protagonist)
Katherine Johnson - Ms. Katherine (Ada's teacher)
Grace Hopper - Grace Hopper Technical High School
